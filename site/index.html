The 100 fastest drivers over one lap

The only fair comparison in F1 is the driver in the other car. Every qualifying session since 1950 is reduced to teammate gaps. Those gaps form one web, linked through every driver who changed teams, and the web is solved all at once. A win over a teammate only counts for as much as that teammate proved against everyone else.
